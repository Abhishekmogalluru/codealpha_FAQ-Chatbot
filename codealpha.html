About Lyra

Deterministic NLP Poetics & Songcraft Engine

Lyra is an offline, client-side reference assistant engineered for the study of literary verse, song lyrics, poetic meters, and classical rhetoric. It executes an entirely deterministic Information Retrieval pipeline mimicking NLTK/SpaCy without remote LLM dependencies or hallucinations.

1. Tokenization & Normalization: Contractions expansion, punctuation stripping, and stopword pruning based on linguistic corpora.
2. Algorithmic Porter Stemming: Reduces morphological affixes to grammatical roots (e.g. poetic, poems → poem; rhymed, rhyming → rhyme).
3. Vector Space (TF-IDF): Maps questions into sparse geometric vectors weighted by term frequency and smoothed inverse document frequency.
4. Cosine Metric & Intent Routing: Measures vector cosine angles, exact phrase matches, and comparative routing (e.g., distinguishing "what is a poem" from "poem vs song").
Curatorial Scope: 18 canon chapters spanning poems, songs, sonnets, ballads, odes, elegies, epic narratives, haikus, verse drama, metrical feet, rhyme types, and prose taxonomy.
